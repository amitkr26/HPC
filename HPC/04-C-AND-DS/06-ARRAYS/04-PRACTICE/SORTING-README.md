## Why it belongs here

Sorting reuses everything from the arrays module: traversal, comparison,
swapping, and loops. After arrays it is the natural next step (Level 4 gap in
`01-ROADMAP.md`).

## Planned order when it is added

1. Bubble sort (simplest - compare neighbours, swap, repeat).
2. Selection sort (find the smallest, place it at the front).
3. Insertion sort (build a sorted prefix).
