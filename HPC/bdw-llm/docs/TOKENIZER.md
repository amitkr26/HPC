## 5. A cluster constraint worth remembering

The first submission (job 31757) requested `--cpus-per-task=8` and sat
PENDING for 15 minutes. The reason:

```
JobState=PENDING Reason=QOSMaxCpuPerUserLimit
ReqTRES=cpu=8,mem=30400M,node=1,billing=12
```

QOS `training` allows **`MaxTRESPU=cpu=4`**. Combined with
`MaxWall=00:15:00` and `MaxSubmitJobs=1`, the per-job ceiling on this
cluster is **4 CPUs, 1 GPU, 15 minutes, one job at a time**. Every later
stage must stay inside those limits or it will never schedule.
